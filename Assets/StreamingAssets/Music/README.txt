Chaos League music folder

Put mp3, ogg or wav files here (subfolders are fine). The game shuffles through them in the
background, and the king can pick one with !song <part of the name> or skip with !skipsong.
!playlist lists them in chat. Songs are named after their file names, so "Artist - Title.mp3"
works well. Use the "Rescan Music Folder" button in settings after adding files.

Only add music you're licensed to play on a stream. Twitch's music rules apply whether or not
the stream earns money. Libraries made for streamers (for example StreamBeats) let you download
tracks for this; check each library's license.

To keep the music somewhere else, set MusicFolder in config.json (absolute, or relative to
StreamingAssets).
